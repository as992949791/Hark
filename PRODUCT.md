# Hark

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Hark helps people discover demand signals in public conversations. People with a product inspect potential users; people investigating a topic inspect unmet needs. Results retain original-source evidence and visible usage costs. The person decides whether to follow up.

## Users

The current deployment is a personal test workspace. Initial real research uses the public CapCut/video-editing case. No customer adoption, acquisition outcome or market-size estimate has been established.

## Operating Context

Users sign in through Clerk, manage product projects and manually start cross-platform research. Product mode can reuse an owned profile; topic mode requires no product. Research presents Chinese explanations and original-language quotes over English public sources. Existing product workflows include Reddit/X leads, sources, filters, competitors, Insights, Reddit SEO, notifications and read-only API/MCP.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: TypeScript, Next.js, React, Tailwind, Base UI, Lucide, PostgreSQL and Drizzle.
- Cross-platform research combines Reddit, X and web-search-discovered pages. Contextual review filters instructional/recommendation/promotion sources, combines repeated needs with supporting quotes, and qualifies concrete product requirements. Explicit saved-original reuse preserves collection/expiry times and avoids repurchasing the corpus.
- Saved real CapCut sources have completed topic analysis and new-URL page/profile preparation through the actual local worker/UI chain. The final topic run has one case with four quotes from two posts by one observed author, labelled an individual theme. This is reused-source analysis, not a fresh full search. Exact-source checks do not establish relevance; broader human 20-result/16-relevant acceptance remains pending.
- The reviewed research release is published on the existing Netlify/Neon personal test site. Cloud data is preserved; local research runs are not automatically synchronized. Paid/live/background/scheduled controls and automatic notifications remain disabled. Open-source software does not make data/model requests free.
- Email and generic Webhook have been verified. Slack/Discord receipt and wallet OAuth remain unverified; wallet work is deferred.
- Preserve Clerk, owner isolation, stored records and existing URLs. No automatic comments or direct messages. Saving a monitor never enables it automatically.
- Daily monitors save product/topic conditions, an owned email channel and finite run/lifetime budgets. They start paused; a baseline precedes reminders about newly observed source/quotation evidence. Workarounds and limitations reuse the final review call and retain exact source spans; missing evidence stays unknown. The interfaces are publicly deployed with all paid/recurring controls off. One real saved-CapCut analysis validated a grounded limitation and kept the unstated workaround unknown; this is not fresh-source monitoring or general model-quality acceptance.
- Illustrative homepage/device previews remain labelled examples.
