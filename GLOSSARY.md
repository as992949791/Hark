# Hark domain glossary

- **Project**: a user's product URL, its product facts, configuration and discovery results.
- **Product profile**: website-derived facts used to find and judge potential buyers. Unknown facts may remain empty.
- **Product brief**: market distinctions and example needs supplied to Jev beside the profile.
- **Evaluation**: a candidate's judgement against a particular profile version, including rejection and review decisions.
- **Lead**: a qualified candidate persisted for the user's feed.
- **Profile trace**: a private saved page plus model attempts, their responses or error kinds, and durations.
- **Replay**: processing recorded responses through current profile validation without contacting providers or writing project data.
- **Archive**: a commit and annotated version tag whose GitHub references match local Git objects.

## Cross-platform discovery

**Demand signal**: a person's public expression of a concrete difficulty, request for a tool recommendation, or reason for seeking an alternative. A signal can inform research without qualifying as a lead for a particular product.

**Demand case**: One concrete need attributed to one observed author or, when the author is unknown, one source document. Repeated expressions may support the same case without becoming additional people or requests.

**Product qualification**: Whether known product facts satisfy a demand case's expressed requirements. Shared audience alone is insufficient; unsupported requirements remain unknown and explicit conflicts prevent a positive match.

**Demand theme**: a recurring problem supported by demand signals from independent people. A single person's feedback remains an individual case.

**Demand run**: One explicitly requested investigation of needs around a product or a topic, together with its collected evidence and results.
_Avoid_: Scan when referring to the separate existing product monitoring workflow.

**Source document**: Public content actually retrieved from its original source. A search snippet is a discovery hint, not the full source document.
_Avoid_: Google post, when Google only found somebody else's page.

**Observed author**: The author identity supported by the source material. Distinct observed identities are evidence about the retrieved sample, not verified distinct people or market size.

**Individual case**: A demand finding supported by only one identifiable author, or by material whose authors cannot be distinguished. It does not establish a recurring theme.
